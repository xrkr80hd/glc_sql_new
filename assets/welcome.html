Welcome to Liberty Church. We’re so glad you’ve found your way here. At Liberty, our desire is simple — to know Jesus, grow in His Word, and share His love with our community and beyond.

Whether you’ve been walking with the Lord for years, are just beginning to explore faith, or you’re somewhere in between, you are welcome here. We believe church is more than a building — it’s a family.

Every Sunday, we gather to worship, learn from God’s Word, and encourage one another to live out our faith in real, practical ways. We’d love for you to join us, not just as a visitor, but as part of this growing family of believers.

Come as you are, bring a friend, and let’s pursue Jesus together.

Pastor Andrew Stokes
